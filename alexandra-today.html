A place to call home

A question for the present

What makes belonging?

A home can hold family memories, shared routines and a sense of place. What would you want a visitor to understand about the place you call home?

Curatorial reflection, rather than a quotation from a resident.

This exhibition connects dated historical sources with questions for today. It is not a live news feed or a report of current population, services or venue conditions.

Memory in the built environment

Heritage lives
through participation.

In its 2018 account of the Alexandra Heritage Centre, Peter Rich Architects describes interviews with residents and community-made paving panels as part of the project (Peter Rich Architects, 2018).

That account offers one example of how memories and making can become part of a place’s design.
