Enquiries and contributions

Help keep the story open.

Ask about research access, possible historical material, volunteering, partnerships or sponsorship.

Choose the enquiry type that best matches your question. Include enough context for a useful response.

Before sharing material

Keep originals safe. Include ownership, dates and permissions where known. An enquiry does not grant publication rights or guarantee acceptance.
