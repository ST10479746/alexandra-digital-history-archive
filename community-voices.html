Begin with a conversation

What do you
want remembered?

A place, a person or a small detail can be the start of a memory. These prompts can help you describe a possible contribution.

Discuss a memory ↗
A place that mattered

Where did people gather? Describe a street, a home or a meeting place. What do you remember about it, and approximately when?

A person who made a difference

Who taught you, cared for others or brought people together? What contribution would you want others to understand?

A sound, routine or occasion

What do you remember hearing or doing? Explain the setting and separate what you witnessed from what others told you.

Before a story is shared

Care for the person.
Care for the record.

01

Permission

Agree on whether a contribution may be published, how the contributor will be credited and what should remain private.

02

Context

Keep approximate dates, locations and the relationship to the event with the account. Record uncertainty honestly.

03

Consideration

Review sensitive information and other people’s details before publication. Preserve the meaning of the account when editing.

These are the project’s proposed editorial principles. The enquiry form starts a discussion; it does not record, upload or publish an interview.
